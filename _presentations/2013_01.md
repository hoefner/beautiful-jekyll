abstract: "This talk is a continuation of Franck's talk. Model Checking was successfully applied to countless (industrial) case studies, as Franck pointed out. However, several case studies indicate that model checking is limited in some scenarios: state space explosion restricts applicability to large distributed systems, and quantitative reasoning, often sufficient for applications, is not possible. Both deficiencies can be overcome to some extent by the use of statistical model checkers, such as SMC-Uppaal. In this talk I will give a short introduction to statistical model checking and illustrate its usefulness by a quantitative analysis of two well-known routing protocols for wireless mesh networks, namely AODV and DYMO. Moreover, I will report on experiments where we pushed the limits of statistical model-checking and showed that  this technology is capable of analysing networks of up to 100 nodes."
