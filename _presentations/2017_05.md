title: "Split, Send, Reassemble: A Formal Specification of a CAN bus Protocol Stack"
year: 2017
venue_type: contributed
venue: "2nd Workshop on Models for Formal Analysis of Real Systems (MARS 2017)"
location: "Uppsala, Sweden"
pdf: "2017_04_MARS.pdf"
abstract: "We present a formal model for a fragmentation and a reassembly protocol running on top of the standardised CAN bus, which is widely used in automotive and aerospace applications. Although the CAN bus comes with an in-built mechanism for prioritisation, we argue that this is not sufficient and provide another protocol to overcome this shortcoming."
