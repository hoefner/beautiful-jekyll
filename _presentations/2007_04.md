title: "Light-Weight Formal Methods with Heavy-Weight Automation —or— One Year in Sheffield is not enough"
year: 2007
venue_type: seminar
venue: "University of Sheffield"
location: "Sheffield, UK"
pdf: "2007_02_sheffield.pdf"
abstract: "It has often been claimed that model checking, special purpose automated deduction or interactive theorem proving are needed for formal program development. We demonstrate that off-the-shelf automated proof and counterexample search is an interesting alternative if combined with the right domain model. As a result we show that variants of Kleene algebras might provide light-weight formal methods with heavy-weight automation. The first part of the talk will focus on case studies where this approach was successfully applied, including Hoare logic, concurrency control and refinement calculus. The second part outlines how this can be extended to an algebraic verification environment for programs and software systems. Since there is a lot more to do it shows that (for me) one year in Sheffield was not enough."
