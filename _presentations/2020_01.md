abstract: "I joint ANU about 1 month ago. It is time to introduce myself and to present my current and future work, as well as my vision, which is to apply formal methods to real world problems and push them to (beyond) their limits. In this talk I will give a short overview over past/future projects, stretching from protocol verification to algebraic models of program logics. The presentation will also include a discussion on cooperation and merging synergies. Nearly no technical details will be given in the talk; however, I will use this talk to advertise potential future talks. These talks will, in case there is enough interest, present technical details on a particular topic/project."
