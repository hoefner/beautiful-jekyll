<p>Peter is the elected Vice Chair of the <a href="https://www.ifip.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">International Federation for Information Processing (IFIP) Technical Committee 2</a> — Software: Theory and Practice, an international body comprising 17 Working Groups and over 450 researchers and ICT professionals. He served as elected Chair of <a href="https://ifipwg21wiki.cs.kuleuven.be/IFIP21/WebHome" target="_blank" rel="noopener">IFIP Working Group 2.1</a> on Algorithmic Languages and Calculi from 2020 to 2025, and is an elected member of <a href="https://ifip-tc2-wg23.paluno.uni-due.de" target="_blank" rel="noopener">IFIP Working Group 2.3</a> on Programming Methodology.</p>
<p>He serves on the editorial board of the <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-logical-and-algebraic-methods-in-programming" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Journal of Logical and Algebraic Methods in Programming (JLAMP)</a> and on the steering committees of <a href="https://ramics-conference.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">RAMiCS</a> and <a href="https://mars-workshop.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">MARS</a> — the latter of which he founded in 2015. He is also a member of the Formal Methods Europe (FME) Industry Committee.</p>
